Growth Rate and Living Standards

GDP per capita: $\color{red}{\frac{Y}{N}}$, then its growth rate: $\color{red}{\gamma_{Y/N}= \gamma_Y - \gamma_N}$

$$\gamma_Y = 2\%$$ $$\gamma_N \approx 1\%$$

Thus,

$$\color{red}{\gamma_{Y/N} \approx 1\%}$$

How long will it take GDP per capita to double at a rate of 1$\%$?

$$ \frac{Y}{N} \times (1+\gamma_{Y/N})^T = 2\times \frac{Y}{N}$$

$$ T = \frac{\log(2)}{\log(1+0.01)} \approx \color{red}{70~\textrm{ years}} $$
